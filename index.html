01 — Commissions

Photography for people, occasions and businesses

Portraiture and photographic narratives are the specialities, circled on the contact sheet below, alongside weddings, parties, location shoots and commercial work.

  • ▸ 01

    Speciality

    Portraits

    Considered, unhurried portraiture that captures the character of the person in front of the camera.

  • ▸ 02

    Speciality

    Photographic narratives

    Stories told across a series of pictures, following a place, a trade or a community for features and personal projects.

  • ▸ 03

    Weddings

    The whole day as it unfolds, from the quiet moments before the ceremony to the last of the celebrations.

  • ▸ 04

    Parties

    Birthdays, anniversaries and family celebrations, photographed with an eye for the people and the moments between them.

  • ▸ 05

    Location

    Shoots wherever the subject lives or works: in the street, at home, in the workplace or much further afield.

  • ▸ 06

    Commercial

    Photography for businesses, brands and publications: the people, the places and the work behind them.

02 — About

From East End boxing halls to imperial palaces, flower markets to mountain tops and beyond.

Jeremy Freedman Photographic Ltd is run by Jeremy Freedman, a photographer based in Spitalfields, in London’s East End, who specialises in portraiture and photographic narratives.

His pictures have been featured by The Guardian, the BBC, Time Out, easyJet magazine and Spitalfields Life. Alongside editorial work, he takes on portraits, weddings, parties, location shoots and commercial commissions for private clients and businesses.
